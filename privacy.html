Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Meetbound collects through our website and Meetbound AI, why, and what we do with it. We keep it to the minimum we need.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, used to sign you in and contact you about your account.
  • Usage records: your plan, how many answers you've used, which tool you used and when. We don't keep the text you type or the answers generated.
  • Payment records: your subscription ID and status from Razorpay. Card, UPI and bank details are handled entirely by Razorpay; we never see or store them.
  • Enquiries: if you email us or use the contact form on our home page, the details you send.

What stays on your device

The "About you" details and form drafts you type into Meetbound AI are saved in your own browser's local storage so you don't have to retype them. They aren't sent to us except when you click Write it. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Who processes your data

  • Anthropic (the Claude AI): the text you submit is sent to generate your answer.
  • Supabase: stores your account and usage records and sends sign-in emails.
  • Razorpay: processes payments and subscriptions.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.

These providers may process data outside India, under their own security and privacy commitments. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and usage records while your account is open, and payment records as long as tax and accounting law requires. Ask us to delete your account and we'll do it within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing meetboundsales@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and only our server can read account records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date above.
