FAQ

Questions

Anything else? Write to meetboundsales@gmail.com.

Who is this for?

Salespeople, SDRs, founders and freelancers who sell to businesses and want better cold calls, emails and LinkedIn messages without spending an hour on each one.

What counts as one answer?

Each time you click Write it and get a result. Asking again for a different version uses another answer. If something goes wrong on our side, the answer isn't counted.

Will the messages sound like a robot?

They're written to sound like a person: short, specific and about the buyer's problem. Always read and adjust before sending, especially names and numbers. The AI won't invent facts; it leaves [placeholders] for you to fill.

Do you store what I type?

No. Your text is sent to the AI to write your answer and isn't saved on our servers. We only store your email, your plan and how many answers you've used. Your "About you" details stay in your own browser.

How do I cancel?

Sign in, then click "Cancel renewal" next to your plan. You keep your answers until the end of the month you paid for.

Can Meetbound do the outreach for me?

Yes. If you'd rather have qualified meetings booked into your calendar, see our done-for-you outbound service.
