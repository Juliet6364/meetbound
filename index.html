How we qualify

Every meeting arrives qualified, with a brief.

We use MEDDICC, the checklist enterprise sales teams use to tell real deals from polite interest. Before a meeting reaches your calendar, we confirm the basics. The brief then tells you exactly what's still open, so your first call is a real discovery call, not a guessing game.

FAQ

Questions founders ask

Anything else? Ask on the call.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A call that actually happens with a decision-maker or strong influencer at a company that fits your ideal customer profile, who has agreed to talk about your product. Before booking, we confirm a real problem, the budget owner, what the problem costs and what they use today. No-shows and wrong-fit calls don't count toward the target.

Will outreach go out in our name?

Yes. Messages go out in your company's name, and you approve every message before outreach starts.

Which markets do you cover?

India first, plus the US, UK, Middle East and Southeast Asia, for B2B IT products, SaaS and software services sold to mid-sized and enterprise companies.

Do we need to buy any tools?

Usually not for the pilot. If a paid tool like LinkedIn Sales Navigator helps, it's billed at cost, only with your approval.

Do you guarantee sales?

We guarantee meetings, not closed deals. We bring qualified buyers to the table, and closing stays with you, where it belongs.

Can we stop anytime?

The pilot is one month. Monthly plans can be stopped with 30 days' notice.
