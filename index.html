FAQ

Questions founders ask

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A call that actually happens with a decision-maker or strong influencer at a company that fits your ideal customer profile, who has agreed to talk about your product. No-shows and wrong-fit calls don't count.

Will outreach go out in our name?

Yes. Messages go out from your company's name, and you approve every message before outreach starts.

Which markets do you cover?

India first, plus the US, UK, Middle East and Southeast Asia for B2B software sold into mid-sized and enterprise companies.

Do we need to buy any tools?

Usually not for the pilot. If a paid tool like LinkedIn Sales Navigator helps, it's billed at cost with your approval.

Can we stop anytime?

The pilot is one month. Monthly plans can be stopped with 30 days' notice.
