Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

The short versionCancel anytime from your account. You keep your answers until the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. We refund duplicate charges and charges for a service we couldn't deliver.

Free plan

Every new account gets 3 free answers. No payment details are needed.

Cancelling a paid plan

  • Sign in to Meetbound AI and click Cancel renewal next to your plan, or email meetboundsales@gmail.com from your account email.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. Your plan and remaining answers stay active until the end of the current paid month.
  • There are no cancellation fees.

Refunds

Monthly plans are low-cost and start working the moment you pay, so we don't usually refund part-used months. We will give a full refund for that month if:

  • you were charged twice, or charged after cancelling;
  • a technical problem on our side stopped you using the service for most of the month; or
  • you ask within 48 hours of your first payment and have used fewer than 5 answers from the paid plan.

To request a refund, email meetboundsales@gmail.com with your account email and payment date. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Razorpay will retry it. If it keeps failing, your plan ends at the end of the paid month and your account returns to the free plan.
